    Core syllabus

    Exam domains

    The Claude Certified Associate (Foundations) exam covers 7 domains across 30 task statements. There is a study lesson for every one of them, plus four weighted practice papers.

    The real exam is 60 items in 120 minutes and costs $99 USD per attempt, delivered through Pearson VUE either online-proctored or at a test centre. The pass mark is a scaled score of 720 on a 100–1000 range, and the credential is valid for 12 months. Registration runs through the Anthropic Partner Academy. Figures are from Exam Guide v1.0, effective July 2026 — check the official guide before you book, since Anthropic can revise the blueprint without notice.

    Study lessons

    Thirty lessons, one per blueprint task statement. Each one teaches the idea, sets out the wrong options the examiner reuses, checks your understanding with a worked item, and gives you a hands-on exercise to run on your own work. Progress is tracked in your browser as you go.

    Practice papers

    Four papers, 180 items, no repeats between them. Every item carries a rationale for each option and a key idea, so a wrong answer teaches the principle rather than just the letter. Papers are weighted to the blueprint, so the domain breakdown on your score report is a fair read of where you stand.

    How to use this

    1. Sit Paper A cold. The score matters less than the domain breakdown at the end.
    2. Take your two weakest domains and work through their lessons — including the exercises, not just the reading.
    3. Sit Paper B. If a domain is still under 70%, the gap is conceptual, not careless.
    4. Sit Mock Exam 1 under exam conditions: 120 minutes, one sitting, no notes.
    5. Review every incorrect item, read the rationale for the options you did not pick, and re-read the lesson behind any domain that is still weak.
    6. Sit Mock Exam 2 as a final rehearsal a few days before the real thing.
