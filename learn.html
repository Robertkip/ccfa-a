    Curriculum

    Learn

    Thirty lessons, one for every task statement in the CCAO-F blueprint. Each teaches the idea, names the wrong answers the examiner reuses, checks your understanding, and gives you something to do with it at work.

    Work them in blueprint order or start with your weakest domains from a practice paper. Every lesson carries a key concept, a comparison table, three exam traps, a check question with rationales for each option, and a hands-on exercise. Progress is saved in this browser only — it is not sent anywhere and will not follow you to another device.

    How the lessons and the papers fit together

    1. Sit Practice Paper A cold. Ignore the score; take the domain breakdown.
    2. Work the lessons for your two weakest domains, doing the exercises rather than only reading.
    3. Drill that domain's bank items on its domain page until the rationales stop surprising you.
    4. Sit Practice Paper B. A domain still under 70% is a conceptual gap, not carelessness.
    5. Work the remaining lessons in blueprint order, heaviest domains first.
    6. Sit Mock Exam 1 under real conditions, then Mock Exam 2 as a final rehearsal.
