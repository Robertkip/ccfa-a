    Learning resources

    Start with the official exam guide and Anthropic's own free training. Everything else on this page is supplementary, and community material should never be your only source for a fact about the exam.

    Official and primary sources

    • Official · start here Claude Certification Program — Pearson VUE

      Where the exam is delivered and scheduled, online-proctored or at a test centre. Covers the three certification roles, the Credly badging arrangement, and the rescheduling rules. Registration itself runs through the Anthropic Partner Academy.

    • Official Anthropic Academy — free course catalogue

      The free training catalogue behind the certification program. For the Associate track the relevant courses are the AI fluency material, Claude 101 on the product side, and anything covering Projects, artifacts and responsible use. The developer-heavy courses on the API and MCP are aimed at the Developer and Architect tracks, not this one.

    • Official Prompt engineering overview — Anthropic docs

      The primary reference behind Domain 1. Written for developers, but the techniques — being explicit, giving examples, letting the model reason before it concludes — are exactly what the Associate exam tests in a business framing.

    • Official Claude Help Center

      The authoritative source for product behaviour: Projects, artifacts, research mode, connectors, and the privacy and data-handling articles that sit behind Domains 5 and 6. If a community guide contradicts this, this wins.

    • Official Anthropic news

      Worth a skim before you sit. Product features move, and an exam guide effective July 2026 can describe a product surface that has since changed.

    Six-week study plan

    Assumes you already use Claude for work a few times a week. If you do not, add two weeks of simply using it on real tasks before you start — this exam is difficult to pass on theory alone.

    • Week 1 — Diagnose. Read the official exam guide end to end. Sit Practice Paper A cold, without studying. Ignore the score; write down your two weakest domains from the report.
    • Week 2 — The heavy domain. Output Evaluation is 21% of the exam, more than any other. Work its six lessons, then its 38 bank items in drill mode, and read the Help Center articles on how Claude handles sources and uncertainty.
    • Week 3 — Prompting and troubleshooting. Domains 1 and 7 together are 24% and share a mental model: diagnose before you correct. Work the four Domain 1 lessons and the three Domain 7 lessons, read the prompt engineering overview, then drill both domains.
    • Week 4 — Product, configuration, workflow. Domains 3, 4 and 5 are 40% combined and are the most product-specific. Work their thirteen lessons and do the exercises rather than only reading — build a real Project with instructions and knowledge as you go, because the questions are much easier once you have configured one.
    • Week 5 — Governance and full rehearsal. Work the four Domain 6 lessons, then sit Mock Exam 1 under real conditions: 120 minutes, one sitting, no notes, no interruptions.
    • Week 6 — Close the gaps and rehearse again. Review every incorrect item and read the rationales for options you did not choose. Sit Practice Paper B and Mock Exam 2. Book the real exam when both papers clear 80%.

    Before you book

    • Confirm the current exam guide version. Domain weights, fees and policies can change without notice, and this site was built against v1.0 effective July 2026.
    • Check your registration route. Registration runs through the Anthropic Partner Academy, which is oriented to Claude Partner Network member organisations — confirm you have access before planning a sitting date.
    • Decide test centre or online proctored. Online proctoring has room, equipment and identity requirements worth reading in advance rather than on the day.
    • Note the retake rules: waiting periods of 14, 30 and 90 days after failed attempts, up to four attempts in a rolling twelve months, with the fee payable each time.
    • Diarise renewal. The credential is valid for 12 months and on-time renewal is a non-proctored assessment; letting it lapse means sitting the full exam again at full fee.
